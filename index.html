Who I Am

Where Clinical Meets Technology

A registered nurse who crossed the bridge into healthcare IT — uniquely positioned to speak both the language of clinicians and the language of systems.

🌉

The Bridge Story

I joined Al Salamah Hospital as one of the first nursing staff and a Cerner Millennium Superuser, leading the clinical content setup for HIS across medical, nursing, and allied health teams. After successfully delivering that project, I was invited to transition into the ICT Department as a Clinical Application Analyst — becoming the critical bridge between clinical teams and technology vendors. Over the past four years I've grown into a Jira Administrator, integration developer, and clinical informatics specialist, certified as a CPHIMS by HIMSS.

🏥

Clinical Background

BSc Nursing from Cairo University (Tanta Faculty). Hands-on experience across Pediatric ICU, Emergency Department, and clinical education — spanning Egypt, UAE, and Saudi Arabia. Clinical Instructor in Medical-Surgical Nursing and Pharmacology.

Technology Edge

Builds Node.js integration services, designs Oracle-to-Jira workflows, administers enterprise ITSM platforms, and creates AI-assisted tools using the Anthropic API. Comfortable working across SQL Server, Oracle, REST APIs, and clinical HIS platforms.
